Added safe SDK scalar and error conversions for nil, bool, i64, f64, UTF-8, bytes, invalid-argument errors, and extension errors. Encoders retain backing storage; host-only decoders copy into owned values and fail closed on malformed tags, reserved fields, booleans, pointers, lengths, UTF-8, codes, and empty errors. Migrated the loader off its one-off decoder. No upstream changes were needed.
